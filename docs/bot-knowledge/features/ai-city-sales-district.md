# AI City Sales District (District 5: Growth & Acquisition)

## Architectural Purpose
Visually models the 24/7 Autonomous Sales & Acquisition Workforce within the **REVORA AI City** 3D simulation. Founders and clients can see the real-time heartbeat of business growth happening around the clock.

---

## 3D Architectural Zones

### 1. The Scout Tower & Radar Dish
- **Visual Landmark**: A sleek, golden-framed communications spire topped with an animated low-poly radar dish sweeping across the digital horizon.
- **Realtime State**:
  - **Idle/Scanning**: Soft cyan pulsing beacon.
  - **Active Prospect Discovery**: High-frequency amber sweep beam indicating live directory analysis.
  - **Qualified Lead Found**: A glowing data packet travels from the tower to the Demo Forge via elevated cybernetic conduits.

### 2. The Demo Forge Studio
- **Visual Landmark**: An open-air glass manufacturing pavilion with a floating holographic wireframe of a website being built in real time.
- **Realtime State**:
  - Lights switch between warm gold (Sol designing layout) and crisp electric blue (Terra running structural audit).
  - When a private demo (`/p/:token`) completes, a finished miniature 3D tablet model illuminates and enters the Outreach Vault.

### 3. The Deal Lounge & Founder HUD
- **Visual Landmark**: A rooftop observation deck with panoramic neon city views and interactive holographic displays.
- **Realtime State**:
  - Displays the live **Jarvis Mobile Approval Queue** count.
  - Glows emerald green when a prospect books a walkthrough or signs up.
  - Tap/click opens the mobile dialer teleprompter and dossier for assisted calling.

### 4. The Voice Reception Spire
- **Visual Landmark**: A cylindrical audio-wave spire that pulses with dynamic soundwave geometries.
- **Realtime State**:
  - Lights illuminate with an organic voice wave animation whenever the inbound AI receptionist is on a call with a prospective client.

---

## Performance & iPhone 17 Optimization
- **Instanced Geometry**: All structural beams, conduits, and neon trim share single material draw calls.
- **Baked Ambient Occlusion**: Shadows and subtle surface gradients are pre-baked, keeping GPU compute purely focused on dynamic particle beacons.
- **Fluid Touch Navigation**: Pinch-to-zoom and two-finger pan optimized for iOS Safari with 60 FPS capped rendering.
