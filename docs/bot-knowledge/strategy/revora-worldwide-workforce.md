# REVORA Worldwide: The AI Workforce That Replaces Business Busywork

## 1. Core Commercial Promise & Strategic Position
REVORA is positioned as a **unified autonomous AI workforce and business operating system** for local businesses, service companies, independent professionals, and scaling enterprises worldwide—not merely a website builder or collection of chat prompts.

### The Central Problem
Business owners are crushed by software sprawl, juggling 10–20 fragmented subscriptions, agency fees, freelancers, spreadsheets, and disconnected inboxes costing **$1,200–$2,800/month** ($14,400–$33,600/year).

### The Unified Solution
**One platform. One connected business system. An AI workforce that eliminates repetitive digital labor and SaaS sprawl.**
- **Canonical Pricing:** $350 one-time setup (first month free, 3-day full-access trial), then $100/month ongoing.
- **Distinction:** Included with REVORA at no extra platform fee vs. third-party pass-through costs (e.g., SMS/carrier telephony, ad spend, payment processing interchange).

## 2. Workforce Functional Roles & Verification Mandate
Every agent acts as an autonomous specialist with defined tools, permissions, and validation obligations. Jarvis orchestrates; specialists execute:

1. **Jarvis (AI Operations Director):** Central orchestrator. Reviews business health, delegates tasks, requests owner approval for sensitive actions, verifies execution, and writes audit logs.
2. **Astra (Lead Intake & Sales Support):** Captures incoming inquiries, scores urgency, drafts personalized prospect outreach, and advances CRM stages.
3. **Sol (Content & Sales Creative):** Crafts conversion copy, bespoke service pages, proposals, email sequences, social copy, and brand assets.
4. **Luna (Discoverability & Reputation):** Manages local SEO, schema metadata, review-generation pipelines, directory NAP consistency, and search rankings.
5. **Terra (Adversarial QA & Optimization):** Audits funnel friction, checks WCAG AA compliance, inspects responsive wrapping, tests automations, and emits defect directives.
6. **Flare (Campaigns & Audience Growth):** Directs social calendars, promotional campaign assets, seasonal drops, and audience-growth creative.

**The Golden Operating Rule:** No agent may report that an email was sent, a booking confirmed, a review published, or a defect resolved unless the underlying system or API returns positive verification.

- **E. Booking, Scheduling & Job Management:** Interactive calendar pickers, deposit authorization holds, automated reschedule flows, reminder notifications.
- **F. Reviews, Reputation & Customer Trust:** Post-service automated review request invitations, AI review-reply drafting, negative feedback interception.

- **H. Email, SMS & Campaign Automation:** Welcome sequences, win-back campaigns, seasonal announcements, lead nurturing drips.
