# 200-Capability Operating System Catalog & Growth Loops

## Strategic Imperative
Deliver a complete business operating system that solves day-to-day business workflow, communication, and management problems across 25 operational domains, powered by AI generation once, followed by zero-marginal-cost deterministic code execution.

## The 6 Reusable Product-Growth Loops
1. **Loop 1: Templates -> Successful Customers -> Better Templates**  
   Each successful business setup refines the default schemas, copy, and checklists for that niche.
2. **Loop 2: Customer Portals -> Exposure -> New Inbound Clients**  
   End-clients who approve estimates and view portals experience Revora's polish and inquire for their own business.
3. **Loop 3: Partners -> Deployments -> Reusable Knowledge**  
   Agency partners deploy client sites and contribute back reusable workflows and presets.
4. **Loop 4: Support -> Knowledge -> Product Hardening**  
   Support questions automatically turn into knowledge articles and UI simplifications.
5. **Loop 5: Benchmarks -> Routing -> Lower Delivery Cost**  
   Continuous routing optimization selects the lowest-cost model for each job complexity tier.
6. **Loop 6: Successful Workflow -> Adjacent Expansion**  
   A customer adopting the website builder naturally activates estimates, then crew work orders, then client portals.
