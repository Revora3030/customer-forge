# Proactive Retention & Expansion Engine

## Architectural Purpose
Acquiring customers is only half the battle. To maximize Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and reduce churn to near zero, REVORA employs a proactive post-onboarding intelligence engine.

---

## Core Automation Systems

### 1. 24/7 Site Health & Uptime Sentinel
- **Automated Synthetic Checks**: Periodically validates that customer sites respond in <500ms, all form submission endpoints return 200 OK, SSL certificates are valid, and images render without 404s.
- **Self-Healing Directives**: If a broken external link or missing DNS entry is detected, Jarvis alerts the workspace owner with an automated 1-click repair suggestion.

### 2. Autonomous Weekly Growth Brief (Luna Report Card)
- **Weekly Digest Delivered via Email**: Luna aggregates weekly unique visitors, form conversions, lead acquisition cost, and local search position movement.
- **Plain English Insights**: "Your mobile detailing booking form converted 8 new appointments this week, up 25% from last week. Your top keyword 'ceramic coating near me' moved up 3 spots."
- **Proactive Optimization Suggestion**: Sol drafts one recommended high-converting seasonal promo or service expansion ready for the owner to publish with 1 click.

### 3. Natural Expansion & Upsell Mechanics
- **Automated Feature Recommendations**: When a customer crosses 50 leads in their CRM, Jarvis recommends adding automated SMS appointment reminders or Google Calendar two-way sync.
- **Stripe Customer Portal Integration**: Seamless one-click upgrades handled directly inside the customer's workspace settings without manual invoicing overhead.

### 4. Churn Early-Warning Radar
- Detects declining site traffic or stalled lead engagement.
- Jarvis initiates a proactive check-in sequence proposing fresh visual assets, new hero copy, or a seasonal refresh to reignite conversion momentum.
