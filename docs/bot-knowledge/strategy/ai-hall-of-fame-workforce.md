# REVORA AI Hall of Fame: The Complete 24/7 Workforce Blueprint

Strategic blueprint organizing REVORA as an autonomous enterprise where Jarvis acts as the Chief of Staff executive operating system, coordinating all 44 active Lovable models (32 core production models + 12 specialist expansion models) across 16 specialized functional departments.

## 1. Executive Organizational Structure
- **Founder (God Body)**: Strategy, company ownership, financial authority, production-release approval, emergency pause.
- **Jarvis (AI Chief of Staff)**: Plans assignments, dispatches tasks, coordinates departments, tracks progress, and escalates consequential actions.
- **Sol (Creative & Engineering)**: Product design, system architecture, UI layout, code authoring, and innovation.
- **Terra (Independent Assurance)**: Adversarial review, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, contrast/overflow QA, risk analysis, and quality gates.
- **Luna (Operations & Throughput)**: Triage, structured workflows, metadata/SEO, routine queue operations, and monthly spend caps ($100).
- **Multimedia Studio**: High-craft imagery, audio transcription, voiceover synthesis, and motion video assets.
- **Shared Platform Infrastructure**: Durable task engine, capability-aware router, connector/tool gateway, persistent memory, audit ledger, and OpenTelemetry-aligned telemetry.

## 4. Hall of Fame Scorecard & Ranking Metrics
Models earn production lead status strictly through measured performance on representative tasks:
- **Task Success Rate**: Met all machine-verified acceptance criteria.
- **Verified Quality**: Validated by automated tests, independent reviewer models, and DOM/browser audits.
- **Rework Rate**: Frequency of repairs required by subsequent workers.
- **Latency & Cost**: Execution duration and token expenditure per completed unit of work.
- **Reliability**: Frequency of timeouts, refusals, schema violations, or truncated JSON.
- **Regression Rate**: Frequency of breaking existing tests or violating invariants.

## 7. Durable 24/7 Autonomous Task Contract
Every background, scheduled, or event-driven task executed by the workforce must carry a durable execution record:
- `task_id`: Unique immutable identifier.
- `department` & `priority`: Responsible operating unit and queue rank.
- `tenant_id`: Mandatory tenant boundary isolation.
- `selected_model` & `fallback_candidates`: Explicit routing list with failover limits.
- `input_references` & `expected_output_schema`: Structured inputs and schema-validated deliverables.
- `budget_cap` & `deadline`: Maximum permitted spend and timeout duration.
- `checkpoint_state`: Periodic progress snapshots to resume without restarting.
- `tool_audit_log`: Exact tool calls, arguments, and responses.
- `verification_evidence`: Independent QA evaluation links before marking complete.
- `outcome_telemetry`: Final status, duration, retry count, and total token cost.
