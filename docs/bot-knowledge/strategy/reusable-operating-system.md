# Reusable Operating System & Worldwide Expansion Blueprint

## Strategic North Star
Revora expands from an AI website builder into an all-in-one AI-assisted business operating platform that builds, operates, serves customers, trains employees, and manages business workflows across industries and international markets.

## Core Architectural Principle: Build Once, Reuse Infinitely (Zero-Marginal-Cost AI)
- **Build with AI**: Models design, draft, adapt, and assemble templates, logic, calculators, and workflows.
- **Execute with Code**: Deployed runtime operations (sorting, math, status transitions, table joins, email triggers, permissions) run purely via ordinary backend code and database constraints without burning AI tokens.
- **Enforceable Tiers**: Strict separation between free deterministic runtime features, included-credit drafting/summaries, and customer-funded advanced metered inference.

## 6 Connected Platform Layers
1. **Foundation**: Tenant isolation, RLS, RBAC, jobs engine, audit logs, universal connectors.
2. **Creation**: Revora Builder, landing pages, interactive primitives, forms, media assets.
3. **Operations**: REVORA Business Workspace, CRM, appointments, projects, work orders, estimates.
4. **Intelligence**: Jarvis AI assistant, lead qualification, summaries, analytics, research.
5. **Ecosystem**: Reusable industry packs, agency workspaces, partner network.
6. **Global Access**: Localization, i18n, regional tax/currency, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, low-bandwidth mode.

## 10 Major Expansion Products Beyond Marketing
1. **Business Workspace**: Centralized jobs, crew coordination, intake, and status boards.
2. **Customer Portal Builder**: Client-facing magic-link portals for project status, approvals, and invoice payments.
3. **Industry App Factory**: Verticalized packs with pre-configured schemas (auto-detailing, cleaning, contractors, tutors, property maintenance).
4. **Knowledge Workspace**: Tenant-scoped, RLS-isolated business documentation and employee SOPs.
5. **Training Studio**: Onboarding checklists, video/guide courses, and employee milestone tracking.
6. **Migration Center**: Frictionless CSV/JSON data importer and field mapper for migrating off legacy CRMs.
7. **Agency Workspace**: Multi-tenant client management for digital agencies and implementation partners.
8. **Localization Studio**: Reusable translation glossaries and localized multi-lingual site generation.
9. **Workflow Studio**: Event-driven automation rules (`lead.created` -> task dispatch -> SMS notification).
10. **Platform Reliability Center**: Real-time error monitoring, synthetic journey checks, and audit trails.

## 10 Priority Implementation Order
1. Current-state capability inventory & deduplication.
2. Shared task, policy, and evidence contracts.
3. Enforced spending and free-only execution controls.
4. Reusable customer portal foundation.
5. Safe import and migration framework.
6. One complete industry application pack (e.g. Mobile Detailing / Trades).
7. Grounded knowledge and support assistant.
8. Critical workflow regression suite.
9. Localization foundation with first verified second language.
10. Partner/agency workspace and delivery documentation.
