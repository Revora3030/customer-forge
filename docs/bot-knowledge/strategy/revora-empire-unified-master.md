## 2. The 5 Strategic Enterprise Layers
1. **REVORA Core:** Parent OS, multi-tenant CRM, AI workforce orchestrator, Cloudflare edge hosting, and Supabase RLS infrastructure.
2. **REVORA Products:** Focused vertical trade micro-SaaS (Missed-Call Recovery, Quote Intelligence, Job Profitability Dashboards).
3. **REVORA Services:** High-ticket agency builds, custom portals, workflow automation, and SEO/GBP implementations ($1,500–$5,000+).
4. **REVORA Commerce:** Digital product factory, trade SOP bundles, estimator spreadsheets, licensed component kits, and prompt packs.
5. **REVORA Ventures:** Separately branded vertical studios, white-label agency desks, and acquired cash-flowing digital properties.

2. **Founder Command Center:** Real-time MRR, cash flow, task board, and Level 4 approvals.
3. **Revenue Leak Detector:** Watchdog for failed Stripe billings, unpaid quotes, and abandoned checkouts.
4. **Customer Success Copilot:** Tracks customer onboarding velocity and alerts before churn occurs.
5. **REVORA Site Sentinel:** Routine synthetic testing of live customer websites (forms, booking, mobile layout, 200 OK).
6. **Software Factory:** Structured pipeline transforming client requests into reusable SaaS tools.
7. **Digital Product Factory:** Automated packaging of trade workbooks, prompt kits, and SOPs.
8. **Trade-Specific OS Engine:** Reusable configurations for 20+ service trades.
9. **Partner & Reseller Portal:** Attribution tracking and commission statements for agency partners.
10. **MCP & Universal Integration Gateway:** Documented endpoints for external AI agents.
11. **Revenue Intelligence Engine:** Real-time unit economics (Contribution = Revenue - Variable Costs).
12. **Website Care Plans Engine:** Automated recurring maintenance, uptime, and backup tracking.
13. **White-Label Operations Desk:** Agency fulfillment backstop without manual founder work.
14. **Business Opportunity Pipeline:** 7-stage investment gate (Idea -> Validation -> Case -> Prototype -> Pilot -> Launch -> Scale/Stop).
15. **Business Simulation Engine:** Pre-build economic modeling in Jarvis (break-even, CAC payback, downside risk).
