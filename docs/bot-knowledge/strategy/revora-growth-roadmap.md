Revora business model and scaling milestones based on verified canonical offer ($350 setup + $100/mo, 3-day trial):

## Revenue Scenarios ($100/mo + $350 setup)
- 10 customers: $1,000 MRR ($12k ARR) + $3,500 setup fees. Proof stage.
- 50 customers: $5,000 MRR ($60k ARR) + $17,500 setup fees. Unit economics validated.
- 100 customers: $10,000 MRR ($120k ARR) + $35,000 cumulative setup fees. First full repeatable engine.
- 250 customers: $25,000 MRR ($300k ARR). Scaled operations with Jarvis autonomous support.
- 500 customers: $50,000 MRR ($600k ARR). Multi-industry reach.
- 1,000 customers: $100,000 MRR ($1.2M ARR). Established SaaS platform.

## Strategic Priorities
1. The Defensible Moat: Not generic AI page generation, but the end-to-end customer engine (Website + Quotes + Booking + CRM + SMS Follow-up + Reviews).
2. Reliability over Model Expansion: Flawless first-time builds, verified site publishing, zero broken pages, and rock-solid approval safety outweigh adding experimental model tiers.
3. First 100 Engine: Autonomous prospect scout + instant bespoke demo generation + mobile approval card + Stripe checkout link.
