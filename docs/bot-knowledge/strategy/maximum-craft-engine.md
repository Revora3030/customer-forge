# Maximum Craft Engine (Anti-Genericity Contract)

## The 2.5/10 Output Ban
No site generated on Revora / Customer Forge may ever regress to sterile, 2015-era landing page templates (static card grids, ubiquitous repetitive CTA buttons on every block, stock copy, or sterile form wrappers).

## Root-Cause Elimination Invariants

1. **Continuous Strategy Propagation (No Discarded Context):**
   - Business analysis insights (buyer objections, trust barriers, qualifying requirements, specific buyer friction, and positioning) must flow intact through every stage:
     `Business Analysis → Creative Concept (Sol) → Architecture → Section Wording → Batch Composition`.
   - Downstream models must never be forced to invent or guess buyer logic from raw category names.

2. **Front-Loaded Grounded Research:**
   - Deep industry research and buyer expectations must be gathered once at the start before architecture and composition begin, cached at the job level, and reused across all pages.
   - Never defer competitor/buyer expectation research to post-composition polishing rounds.

3. **Cohesive Page Narrative Across Batches:**
   - Multi-section composition batches must receive the complete page architectural blueprint and structural metadata of neighboring blocks (alignment, spatial density, elevation, media treatment, typography scale, interactive mechanics).
   - Models must never receive bare string labels like `["section", "section"]`.

4. **Abolition of Prescribed House Recipes:**
   - Eliminate rigid structural defaults: no forced action buttons at the end of every informational block, no hardcoded `maxWidth:680px` or single-column constraints on contact/inquiry areas, no boilerplate typography clamp patterns.
   - Composition must reflect bespoke architectural choices authored by Sol for the business's specific archetype and audience tier.

5. **Mandatory Craft Verification & Self-Critique:**
   - Technical safety and accessibility are prerequisites, but never the sole standard for job completion.
   - Sol and Terra must perform explicit craft evaluation: verifying unique industry-specific mechanics (e.g., interactive estimators, material selectors, comparison sliders, step workflows), high-impact visual rhythm, bespoke micro-interactions, and zero stock phrasing.
   - A draft that passes only syntactic/safety checks while skipping craft verification must never be marked as benchmark-complete.
