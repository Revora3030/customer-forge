# REVORA 24/7 AI Lead-Generation Autopilot Blueprint

A secure, measurable, multi-channel closed-loop acquisition engine designed to run continuously for REVORA Growth Systems and eventually power customer tenant acquisition.

## 1. The Closed-Loop Feedback Engine
1. **Find Opportunities:** Search intent, directories, public signals, referrals, inbound inquiries, and event triggers.
2. **Research & Qualify:** Verify business legitimacy, identify genuine gaps, deduplicate records, and score commercial fit.
3. **Create the Offer:** Automated performance audits, private demo websites, tailored cost savings matrices, or quote consultations.
4. **Convert & Follow Up:** Authorized multi-channel outreach, instant speed-to-lead response (< 5 mins), calendar booking, and CRM handoff.
5. **Learn & Improve:** Track real conversion rates, cost-per-lead, and pipeline revenue to continuously optimize scoring and channel allocations.

## 2. 14 Major Acquisition Channels (300+ Touchpoints)
- **Channel A (REVORA Public Funnel — 35 Touchpoints):** Contact forms, demo bookings, redesign audits, AI automation assessments, interactive ROI calculators, live chat, and voice capture.
- **Channel B (Search & Organic Inbound — 34 Assets):** High-intent service pages, category playbooks, AI-assisted search optimization, and verified technical SEO.
- **Channel C (Local Business Discovery):** Google Places, OpenStreetMap, verified business directories, chambers of commerce, and public registries.
- **Channel D (High-Opportunity Signals — 30 Indicators):** Missing website, broken mobile layout, slow load speed, missing quote/booking forms, hiring spikes, new business filings.
- **Channel E (Governed Outbound Prospecting):** Research-backed personalized emails, contact forms, direct mail, and video audits with mandatory opt-outs.
- **Channels F & G (Directories & Social Presence):** Yelp for Business, Clutch, Upwork, LinkedIn founder content, YouTube teardowns, and community groups.
- **Channels H & I (Paid Media & Strategic Partnerships):** Search/Meta ads with strict spend caps, web designer affiliate networks, and chamber partnerships.
- **Channels J & K (Events & 27 Free Lead-Gen Tools):** Website quality checkers, ROI calculators, quote template generators, and website briefs.
- **Channels L, M & N (Lifecycle Automation, Inbound AI Agents & Event Triggers):** Dormant lead reactivation, missed-call text-back, and real-time triggers for new business registrations.

## 3. 16 Specialized AI Lead-Gen Roles
1. **Jarvis (Lead Operations Orchestrator):** Executive dispatcher coordinating the entire acquisition funnel.
2. **Prospect Discovery Agent:** Identifies potential business candidates from permitted sources.
3. **Research & Verification Agent:** Checks business viability, public contact information, and deduplication.
4. **Lead Qualification Agent:** Scores lead fit (1–100) based on verified gaps and buying urgency.
5. **Opportunity Audit Agent:** Generates factual performance, SEO, and mobile gap breakdowns.
6. **Offer & Strategy Agent:** Selects the highest-converting angle (speed-to-lead, cost reduction, redesign).
7. **Demo Website Agent:** Automatically generates private, interactive demo sites for prospects.
8. **Content & Inbound Growth Agent:** Authors high-intent educational guides and case studies.
9. **Outreach Preparation Agent:** Drafts personalized, compliant outreach messages for owner approval.
10. **Campaign Operations Agent:** Manages paid media experiments and spend controls.
11. **Inbound Conversion Agent:** Answers live website inquiries and handles instant qualification.
12. **CRM & Follow-Up Agent:** Advances pipeline stages and schedules reminders.
13. **Sales Copilot:** Prepares meeting briefs and proposal packages.
14. **Partnerships & Referral Agent:** Manages affiliate invites and partner co-marketing.
15. **Analytics & Optimization Agent:** Measures attribution, CAC, and channel conversion velocity.
16. **Compliance & Security Guardian:** Enforces CAN-SPAM, opt-out suppression lists, and tenant data privacy.

## 4. Governance & Safety Rules
- Zero cold robo-calls or unverified spam.
- Explicit owner authorization required for all external outbound messages.
- Attribution tracking across the entire funnel: `Visitor → Inquiry → Qualified → Quoted → Won`.
