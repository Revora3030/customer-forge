# REVORA: The Master Empire Blueprint

## 1. Five Strategic Portfolio Layers
1. **REVORA Core:** Platform, multi-tenant CRM, AI workforce (Grok bots + Jarvis), and Cloudflare/Supabase infrastructure.
2. **REVORA Products:** Focused vertical SaaS tools (quote recovery, missed-call intake, job profitability).
3. **REVORA Services:** High-ticket agency implementations, custom portals, automation setup ($1,500–$5,000+).
4. **REVORA Commerce:** Digital products, trade SOP bundles, estimator spreadsheets, licensed component kits.
5. **REVORA Ventures:** Independently branded niche agencies, partner networks, and acquired micro-properties.

## 2. Compounding IP & The Reusable Asset Library
- **The Website-to-SaaS Ladder:** Customer builds generate reusable primitives (estimate calculators, before/after sliders, booking widgets) that graduate into standalone widgets, licensed modules, and micro-SaaS subscriptions.
- **REVORA Asset Library:** Catalog of code primitives, trade design systems, automation workflows, copy frameworks, and calculation models with strict licensing and provenance tracking.

## 3. High-Priority Operational Sentinels & Core Additions (Top 15)
1. **Agent Control Plane:** Controlled, auditable gateway (`/api/public/agent/*`) with 4-level authority.
2. **Founder Command Center:** Consolidated view of MRR, cash flow, tasks, approvals, and portfolio health.
3. **Revenue Leak Detector:** Automated monitoring for unpaid quotes, failed Stripe billings, and abandoned forms.
4. **Customer Success Copilot:** Tracks customer onboarding velocity, feature usage, and proactive churn risk.
5. **REVORA Site Sentinel:** Routine automated synthetic tests of live customer sites (forms, booking, mobile layout, HTTP status).
6. **Software Factory:** Repeatable pipeline turning validated client requests into standalone modules.
7. **Digital Product Factory:** Repeatable packaging of trade workbooks, prompt kits, and starter packs.
8. **Trade-Specific OS Engine:** Reusable configurations for high-demand trades (plumbing, HVAC, detailing, roofing).
9. **Partner & Reseller Portal:** Attribution tracking and dashboards for affiliate agencies.
10. **MCP & Universal Integration Gateway:** Documented REST, webhooks, and tool definitions for external agents.
11. **Revenue Intelligence Engine:** Real-time unit economics (CAC, LTV, direct delivery vs AI inference costs).
12. **Website Care Plans:** Recurring maintenance, uptime monitoring, and security backups.
13. **White-Label Operations Desk:** Backstop agency partner delivery without manual founder intervention.
14. **Business Opportunity Pipeline:** Structured validation gates (Idea -> Validation -> Business Case -> Prototype -> Paid Pilot).
15. **Business Simulation Engine:** Pre-build financial modeling in Jarvis (break-even, CAC payback, downside cases).

## 4. Execution Discipline
- Focus first on making the core $350 + $100/mo subscription engine repeatable, reliable, and churn-resistant before launching secondary ventures.
- Never deploy risky or unapproved changes to production; enforce Level 4 founder authorization for all outbound spend, publishing, and customer contact.
