## 2. Coordinated 3-Bot Workforce (Operational Division of Labor)
- **Bot 1 — Chief Architect & Strategy Director:** Audits REVORA, designs UI/UX in Lovable, plans features, maps roadmaps, prioritizes revenue initiatives, coordinates Bots 2 and 3.
- **Bot 2 — Chief Technology & Lead Engineer:** Operates GitHub (branches, commits, PRs, CI, tests), inspects schemas/RLS, fixes bugs, builds SaaS/API tools, enforces security and tests before deployment.
- **Bot 3 — Chief Revenue & Operations:** Researches local businesses, enriches prospect contacts, packages digital assets, prepares campaigns and sales materials, monitors pipeline and customer retention.

## 5. Security & Autonomy Invariants
- All bots route structural mutations through the Agent Control Gateway (`/api/public/agent/*`) or GitHub PRs.
- Level 4 actions (merge, deploy, publish, send, paid builds, billing, delete) strictly require founder approval on `/admin/approvals`.
- No secrets or credentials exposed in code or bot prompts.
- Audit before editing; prove completion with test and runtime evidence.
