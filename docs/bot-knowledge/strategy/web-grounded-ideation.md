# Web-Grounded Dynamic Ideation & Unconstrained Generation

## 1. Unconstrained AI Authority (Never Bound to a Static Library)
The AI team (Sol, Astra, Terra, Luna) is **never restricted to a fixed set of pre-built widgets or rigid templates**.
- Sol authors unique interactive micro-applications, gamified canvases, stateful visualizers, and conversion tools from first principles for each specific business.
- No deterministic template limitations: the models have full authority to craft custom HTML5 canvas games, interactive SVGs, 360° scrubbers, real-time estimators, and finish swappers suited to the customer's industry.

## 2. Autonomous Web Scanning & Environmental Discovery
When given any business category or niche:
1. **Industry Research Pass**: The AI team executes web-grounded research (via Perplexity agent or capability-routed web search) to scan leading global brands, award-winning sites, and top-ranking competitors in that exact vertical.
2. **Psychology & Friction Analysis**: The model identifies what customers in that industry worry about most (e.g. pressure washing = striping & plant damage; custom builder = budget drift & timeline; luxury rentals = hidden deposit fees & vehicle condition).
3. **Conversion Lever Synthesis**: The AI authors custom interactive mechanics addressing those exact friction points (e.g. Kestrel's "Clean this driveway" simulator proving no stripes; Pennwick's 360° construction timeline proving craftsmanship; CarVault's combination lock proving transparent financing).

## 3. Dynamic Archetype Adaptation
- **Home Builders & Architects**: 360° construction progression scrubber, interactive materials/finishes swapper, lot feature toggles, live build estimate pill.
- **Exterior Cleaning & Trades**: Interactive driveway/roof washing canvas, stain hotspot tooltips, live curb appeal gauge (52/100 -> 99/100), 20-second instant quote wizard.
- **Exotic Car Rental & Dealerships**: Interactive key fob unlock trigger, 360° turntable viewer, head-to-head performance drag race, monthly finance combinator.
- **Luxury Apparel & Goods**: Macro texture inspector with tactile pins, kinetic ghosted typography, limited-edition numbered drop reservation widget.
- **MedSpa & Cosmetic Surgery**: Interactive before/after rejuvenation progression, target treatment area selector, consultation package calculator.
- **Commercial & B2B Services**: ROI/payback estimator, service scope configurator, instant proposal generator.

## 4. Mobile Ergonomics & Verification
- All custom interactive tools must be touch-optimized for iPhone (320px–390px, min 44x44px touch targets).
- Submissions connect immediately to the customer's tenant-isolated CRM with verified lead tracking.
