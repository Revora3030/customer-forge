# Autonomous Growth Services & Zero-Cost SaaS Replacement Engine

## Strategic Purpose
A website alone does not guarantee paying customers. While traditional website builders (Wix, Squarespace) abandon the client immediately after page generation, Revora operates as a complete **Autonomous Business Growth Operating System**. Once the site is authored, the AI team transitions from architects into an ongoing client acquisition, conversion, and retention agency.

---

## The 6 Core Post-Launch Growth Services

### 1. Speed-to-Lead & 60-Second Instant Outreach
- **Problem:** 78% of customers buy from the first business that responds. Inquiries left cold for over 5 minutes have a 10x lower close rate.
- **Solution:** Automated instant SMS and email conversational trigger within 30–60 seconds of form submission.
- **Mechanic:** The AI model pool drafts a hyper-personalized response confirming service availability and proposing a quick 3-minute intake call or site inspection.

### 2. Reputation Engine & Automated Review Harvesting
- **Problem:** Local service businesses survive on Google Maps reviews, but owners find asking awkward and inconsistent.
- **Solution:** Automated post-service review request sequence (SMS + email) triggered when a CRM lead status advances to `Completed` / `Won`.
- **Smart Filter:** Directs satisfied clients straight to the Google Business Profile review link; routes dissatisfied feedback to a private owner dispute loop before negative reviews go public.

### 3. Database Reactivation & Dormant Lead Mining
- **Problem:** Established businesses sit on 500 to 5,000 past customers and unclosed inquiries they haven't contacted in months.
- **Solution:** One-click seasonal reactivation campaigns. Clients upload past customer lists (CSV/contacts), and the AI authors customized, low-pressure re-engagement offers.

### 4. Native Booking, Scheduling & Stripe Deposit Collection
- **Problem:** Redirecting clients to external booking software (Calendly, Acuity, Jobber) leaks conversion and causes no-shows.
- **Solution:** Built-in interactive scheduling directly on the customer's domain, synced with Google Calendar, with upfront Stripe deposit holds ($50–$250) to guarantee attendance.

### 5. Google Business Profile (GBP) & Local Map Pack Sync
- **Problem:** Over 60% of local service clicks go to the Google Map Pack (top 3 local map listings), bypassing standard web links.
- **Solution:** Continuous synchronization of site service offerings, service areas, customer photos, and weekly AI-generated promotional updates to the client's Google Business Profile.

### 6. Interactive Estimates & Digital Contract Sign-Off
- **Problem:** Raw email quote requests force back-and-forth negotiations and delay closing.
- **Solution:** Interactive estimate page with good/better/best package tiers, real-time pricing recalculations, touch signature capture, and immediate deposit payment.
