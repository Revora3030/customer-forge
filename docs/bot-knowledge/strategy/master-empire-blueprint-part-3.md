# REVORA Master Empire Blueprint — Part 3 & Multi-Bot Fleet Scaling

## 1. Elastic Multi-Bot Workforce Expansion
- **Initial 3-Bot Pod:** Bot 1 (Architect/Strategy), Bot 2 (Lead Engineer), Bot 3 (Revenue/QA/Ops).
- **Fleet Expansion Model:** Architecture must support scaling from 3 to N specialized bots (e.g., Release Engineer, Database Migration Reviewer, Accessibility Specialist, AI Evaluation Engineer, Security Reviewer, Market Analyst).
- **Actor Registry & Scoped Identities:** Every bot has a registered agent ID, scoped credentials, permitted gateway tools, and an independent review pair.
- **Delegation Protocol:** Standardized task contract with inputs, affected files, test evidence, rollback plan, and Level 4 gates.

## 2. The 12 Master Enterprise Domains
1. **Corporate Infrastructure & Portfolio Registry:** Parent entity, business units, domain holdings, IP licenses, internal venture incubator.
2. **Advanced Sales & Demand Forecasting:** Prospect scoring, proposal objection feedback loop, pipeline aging alerts.
3. **Customer Lifecycle & First-Value Timer:** Measurement from payment to first customer value; unified customer portal.
4. **Product Innovation & Customer Problem Database:** Root-cause problem logging rather than superficial feature requests.
5. **Agent Security Center & Trust Boundaries:** Input trust categorization, prompt-injection defense, one-action emergency pause.
6. **Financial Controls & Product Profitability:** Contribution margin by product, capital allocation stop-loss, AI cost optimizer.
7. **AI Workforce Orchestration & Task Replay:** Central task orchestrator, structured handoffs, replayable execution logs.
8. **Data Ownership & Intelligence Warehouse:** Data lineage, tenant-safe benchmarks, licensed data assets.
9. **Global Expansion & Trade Editions:** Localized formats, regional compliance, international white-label licensing.
10. **Physical-World Operations:** Equipment/fleet logs, job-site documentation, trade "Business-in-a-Box".
11. **Partnerships & Strategic Asset Acquisition:** Agency network, verified integration catalog, capability acquisitions.
12. **Founder Control & Continuity Center:** Emergency kill switch, backup restoration tests, quarterly REVORA Independence Test.

## 3. Permanent Operational Invariants
- Every product must solve a verified problem.
- Every revenue stream must track its own unit economics and gross margins.
- Every customer must receive promised delivery without fabricated claims.
- Every bot action must be auditable and bounded by 4-level authority.
- Reusable IP from every build feeds the REVORA Asset Library.
