# Configure the site-wide AI quality matrix

## Goal
Make the active AI team treat the uploaded screenshots as a quality reference across the whole generated website: cinematic imagery, strong typography, disciplined color, varied page composition, clear conversion paths, and polished mobile behavior—without copying branding or inventing facts.

## Implementation
1. **Create one enforceable creative quality matrix**
   - Encode reference-level requirements for visual identity, typography, imagery, page rhythm, conversion design, mobile composition, and content integrity.
   - Keep the matrix industry-adaptive: the visual language must suit each business rather than force automotive styling everywhere.

2. **Assign the matrix to the active AI team**
   - Sol owns the complete site direction and page-by-page composition.
   - Sunburst owns cinematic hero/editorial imagery; Flare owns supporting service and detail imagery.
   - Terra independently checks visual distinctiveness, conversion strength, consistency, and page completeness.
   - Luna handles metadata and structured details only after the creative work passes.
   - Preserve capability-aware routing, usage controls, and existing specialist fallbacks.

3. **Apply it to every page and every important visual area**
   - Require a deliberate opening, useful body composition, and closing action on core and service pages.
   - Require generated or owner-supplied imagery wherever the AI design marks imagery as necessary.
   - Prevent one generic image, repeated composition, placeholder copy, or empty visual areas from satisfying site-wide quality.

4. **Strengthen acceptance checks**
   - Reject incomplete AI designs instead of replacing them with old patterns.
   - Check all required phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop widths.
   - Add regression tests proving multi-page image coverage, composition variety, reference-quality rules, and specialist responsibilities.

## Technical details
- Extend the canonical AI design contract with typed quality criteria and page-level visual requirements.
- Feed those criteria into first-build creative direction, image-shot planning, page architecture, and materialization checks.
- Replace the remaining first-build free-first image ordering with task-fit routing while preserving the explicit zero-cost safety switch and paid budget controls.
- Remove the remaining consensus/weak deterministic fallback behavior from creative composition paths touched by this flow.
- Validate with focused tests, the full test suite, lint, current preview diagnostics, and responsive browser checks.
