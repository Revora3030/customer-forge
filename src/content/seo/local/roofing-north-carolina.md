## Questions North Carolina owners ask

**Does a North Carolina roofer need a general contractor license?**
When a project costs $40,000 or more, state law requires a general contractor license. We did not find a separate state roofing license, so check with the NC Licensing Board for General Contractors and your local permit office for your jobs.

**Can customers send storm photos?**
The inspection request can be as short or as detailed as you want, and photos help you judge the job before a visit. Ask customers to send photos by text or by replying to your message once they are in touch.

**Should I publish roof prices?**
Many roofers publish a range and explain what moves it, such as size, pitch and material. Final pricing follows an inspection.

**Do you add before-and-after galleries?**
Yes, using your own photos. We do not use stock photos presented as your work.

**Can the site handle storm-season rushes?**
A first-reply automation, one pipeline for every lead and booking against real availability help when many requests arrive at once. They do not add crews, so set a reply window you can meet.
