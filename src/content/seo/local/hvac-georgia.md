**Licensing and your registration number.** Georgia licenses conditioned air contractors through the Secretary of State's State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Conditioned Air Contractors. There are two classes: Class I is restricted to systems up to 175,000 BTU of heating and 60,000 BTU of cooling, and Class II is unrestricted. State rules also require a business performing conditioned air contracting to display its business registration number, written as "GA. REG." followed by the license number, as set out in the rule.
