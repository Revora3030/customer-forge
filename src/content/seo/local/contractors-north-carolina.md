**The $40,000 line.** North Carolina requires a general contractor license for projects costing $40,000 or more, a threshold set in G.S. 87-1 that took effect October 1, 2023. The NC Licensing Board for General Contractors handles licensing, and classifications and limits differ. If your work sits above or near that line, your website should show your license number and classification.
