**Why Alabama is a cooling-and-heating business.** Alabama has a humid subtropical climate: long, hot, humid summers and relatively mild winters (Alabama Office of the State Climatologist). Daily highs above 95°F are common in summer, and winter lows run from about 30°F in the north to above 40°F on the coast. That means no-cool calls drive your summer, no-heat calls spike on the few cold snaps, and maintenance plans fill the gaps. Your site needs a clear path for each one.

**Licensing belongs on your site.** HVAC work in Alabama is licensed through the Alabama Board of Heating, Air Conditioning & Refrigeration Contractors. The Board's active status requires a business name and a $20,000 performance bond, and licenses run on the calendar year and expire December 31 unless renewed. If you hold a license, put the number in your footer and on your service pages. Tell us the details when you onboard, and we place them where customers look.
