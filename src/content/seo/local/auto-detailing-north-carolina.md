# Auto detailing website design in North Carolina

Detailing customers shop with their eyes. They want to see the work, know the packages and prices, and book a slot. A detailer with a clear website gets those customers before they scroll to the next photo. Revora Growth Systems builds that site for North Carolina detailers, whether you run a shop or a mobile van: packages, instant quotes, online booking and follow-up. Setup is $350, the first month is free, then $100/month.

**Packages sell better than hourly rates.** Detailing buyers think in packages: exterior wash and wax, interior deep clean, full detail, paint correction, ceramic coating. Each gets its own page with what is included and what changes the price. Your quote form asks a few questions (vehicle size, condition, add-ons like pet hair or odor removal) and returns a range you set. You receive the details with the lead.

**Mobile or shop, say which.** Mobile detailers need service-area pages that list the neighborhoods and towns they drive to and say plainly that the work happens at the customer's home or workplace. Shop-based detailers need a location page. Revora is remote and never needs an address for a mobile business; your site lists an address only if you have a real storefront.
