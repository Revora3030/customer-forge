# Revora vs Squarespace

Squarespace is known for polished design, and it is a good choice for a business that wants a good-looking site without much effort. Revora is a different kind of product: a done-for-you system for local service businesses that includes quoting, booking, a CRM and follow-up alongside the site. Here is where each one fits.

Squarespace facts come from Squarespace's own pricing page, checked October 31, 2026. Prices are annual-billing monthly equivalents unless noted. Confirm current pricing at squarespace.com before you buy.

## Side by side

| | Squarespace | Revora |
|---|---|---|
| Price (annual billing) | Basic $19, Core $29, Plus $49, Advanced $99 per month. Monthly billing is higher: $25, $39, $65 and $139 | $350 setup, first month free, then $100/month |
| Free trial | Yes, no card (length per Squarespace's site) | 3 days of full access, no card |
| Contract | Annual or monthly billing | Cancel anytime |
| Who builds the site | You, with Squarespace's templates | Generated from your real services, area and pricing, then editable in a visual builder |
| Domain | Annual plans include a free domain for the first year | Custom domain connection and setup is included in the setup fee |
| Forms, contacts, SEO tools, invoicing | Listed on all plans | Forms and a CRM are part of the system |
| Payment processing | Starts at 2.9% plus $0.30 on Basic and Core | Not listed on Revora's pricing page |
| Online booking | Through Acuity Scheduling, which Squarespace sells as a separate subscription from your site plan | Built in, tied to your services and durations |
| Instant quote with a price range you set | Not listed on the pricing page | Built in |
| Scheduled follow-up and review requests | Not listed on the pricing page | Built in |
| Who owns the site and data | Check Squarespace's terms | The website, customer list and data are yours |

## Where Squarespace is the better choice

- **Design.** If looks matter most and you have strong photos, Squarespace makes a refined site with little effort.
- **Price.** Core is $29 a month on annual billing, against $100 a month for Revora after the free first month.
- **A free trial** that lets you try the whole editor before paying.
- **Simplicity.** Forms, contacts, SEO tools and invoicing are on every plan.
- **Content and portfolios.** Photographers, designers and other visual businesses fit well.

## Where Revora is the better choice

- **You do not want to build or maintain pages.** Your site, quotes and booking are generated from your services, area and ranges.
- **Instant quotes and booking on the same site.**
- **Lead follow-up.** A ready-made first-reply automation and scheduled follow-up help keep leads from going quiet.
- **Review requests.** A ready-made automation sends them after you mark a job complete.
- **A pipeline.** Every inquiry, quote and booking sits in one list with a next step.

## The honest math

A year of Squarespace Core is 12 × $29 = $348. Revora's first year is $350 plus 11 months at $100 = $1,450. The extra roughly $1,100 buys the work a builder leaves with you. If you are happy to write the pages, set up the forms and answer leads quickly, Squarespace is the better value. If you lose jobs to slow replies or never find time to follow up, it may not be.

## Common questions

**Can I move my Squarespace site to Revora?** Your content, photos and domain can move over. Custom domain connection and setup is part of the setup fee.

**Will Revora rank higher?** No one can promise a specific Google position. Rankings depend on useful pages, accurate information and real reviews, on either platform.

**Can I try both?** Yes. Squarespace's free trial and Revora's 3-day trial both work without a card.

**Does Squarespace have booking?** Yes, through Acuity Scheduling. Squarespace describes it as a separate subscription from your website plan. Check Squarespace's current scheduling pricing for your needs.

**Try it before you pay.** Revora gives you 3 days of full access with no card. Setup is $350 one time, your first month is free, then it is $100 a month, and you can cancel anytime. [See pricing](/pricing) or [start your system](/get-started).
