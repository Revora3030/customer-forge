# Revora vs a marketing agency

Agencies sell services delivered by people: strategy calls, campaign management, content and reports. Revora sells a system your business runs on: a website, instant quotes, booking, a CRM, follow-up, review requests and analytics. They overlap, but they are not the same thing, and the right choice depends on what you already have.

Agency details below come from each company's own public pages, checked October 31, 2026. Agencies customize their packages, so treat these as published starting points and ask for a written quote.

## What an agency does better

Be honest about this. An agency can do things a $100 system does not:

- **Managed advertising.** If you have a real ad budget and want specialists running Google or Meta campaigns, an agency can outperform a self-running system on paid media.
- **Custom creative.** Video, photography, brand design and long-form content are people work.
- **Strategy for a bigger business.** Multi-location companies and complex offers benefit from a team.
- **Hands-on account management.** Some owners want a person to call.

## What Revora does better for a small service business

- **Cost and clarity.** One setup fee and one monthly fee, and you can cancel anytime.
- **Ownership.** The site, customer list and data are yours.
- **Lead handling built in.** Quotes, booking, first reply, follow-up and review requests are part of the system, not an extra retainer.
- **Speed.** Your site is generated from your real services, area and pricing, and you can try the full system for 3 days first.

## What to ask any provider, including us

1. What is the total cost for the first 12 months, including setup, ads and any add-ons?
2. What is the minimum term, and what does it cost to leave?
3. Who owns the website, domain, ad accounts and customer list?
4. What is delivered each month, and how is it reported?
5. What happens to a lead that arrives at 9 pm?
6. What does the company not do?

No provider can promise a specific Google position. Be careful with anyone who does.

## Common questions

**Can I use both?** Yes. Many owners run ads through an agency and send the traffic to a Revora site, so paid clicks land on a page that can quote, book and follow up.

**Do I get a person at all?** Yes. Support and system management are included in the monthly fee. What is not included is a retainer for work the system's automations already handle.

**What if I already have an agency contract?** Check your term and exit terms before changing anything. A system can sit alongside it until the contract ends.

**Try it before you pay.** Revora gives you 3 days of full access with no card. Setup is $350 one time, your first month is free, then it is $100 a month, and you can cancel anytime. [See pricing](/pricing) or [start your system](/get-started).
