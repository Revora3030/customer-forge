# Revora vs Wix

Wix is one of the best-known website builders, and it is a good tool. If you want to design your own site with lots of control, it may be exactly right. Revora is a different kind of product: a done-for-you system for local service businesses that includes quoting, booking, a CRM and follow-up along with the site. This page lays out where each one fits.

Wix facts below come from Wix's own plans page, checked October 31, 2026. Prices are annual-billing monthly equivalents. Wix says yearly plans are paid in full upfront and prices exclude tax. Confirm current pricing at wix.com before you buy.

## Side by side

| | Wix | Revora |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Light about $17, Core about $29, Business about $39, Business Elite about $159 per month (yearly billing; Wix says prices vary by location) | $350 setup, first month free, then $100/month |
| Contract | Yearly plans are paid upfront; Wix lists a 14-day money-back guarantee | Cancel anytime. 3 days of full access first, no card |
| Who builds the site | You, with Wix's editor and AI tools | Generated from your real services, area and pricing, then editable in a visual builder |
| Custom domain and hosting | Included on all plans | Included |
| Scheduling and online booking | Included on Core and above | Built in, tied to your services and durations |
| Accepting payments | Core and above. Not on Light | Not listed on Revora's pricing page |
| Instant quote with a price range you set | Not listed on the plans page | Built in |
| CRM, scheduled follow-up and review requests | Not listed on the plans page | Built in |
| Local SEO foundation | Wix lists built-in marketing and SEO tools; you write and set up the pages | Local SEO foundation is included in setup |
| Online store | Wix eCommerce: Basic on Core, Standard on Business, Advanced on Business Elite | Not what Revora is built for |
| Who owns the site and data | Check Wix's terms | The website, customer list and data are yours |

## Where Wix is the better choice

- **Price.** A Wix Core plan is about $29 a month. Revora is $100 a month after the free first month.
- **Design control.** If you like building and want to decide every detail, Wix gives you room to.
- **An online store.** If selling products is the main goal, Wix is built for it.
- **A big set of add-ons.** Wix's help center says its App Market has over 800 apps, some of which need a paid upgrade, if you want to assemble your own setup.
- **Money-back window.** Wix lists a 14-day money-back guarantee on its plans page.

## Where Revora is the better choice

- **You do not want to build it.** Your site, quote form and booking are generated from your real services, area and price ranges.
- **Quotes and follow-up matter.** An instant quote, a ready-made first-reply automation and scheduled follow-up are part of one system.
- **One place for every lead.** Forms, quotes and bookings go into one pipeline with a next step.
- **Review requests.** A ready-made automation sends them after you mark a job complete.
- **You are busy on jobs.** The routine steps run without you.

## The honest math

A Wix Core plan for a year is roughly 12 × $29 = $348, paid upfront. Revora's first year is $350 plus 11 months at $100 = $1,450. The difference is about $1,100. What you are paying for is the work Wix leaves with you: writing the pages, setting up the booking and quote tools, handling leads and asking for reviews. If you would do that work happily and well, Wix is the better value. If you would not, or it would not get done, the system may pay for itself in recovered jobs. Only you can say how many leads you lose now.

## Common questions

**Can I move my Wix site to Revora?** Your content, photos and domain can move over. Custom domain connection and setup is part of the setup fee.

**Will Revora rank higher than my Wix site?** No one can promise a specific Google position. Rankings depend on useful pages, accurate information and real reviews, on either platform.

**Can I use Wix and Revora together?** You can keep a Wix site while you try Revora's 3-day trial. Running both long term usually means two sets of pages to maintain.

**Does Wix have a CRM?** The plans page we reviewed does not list one. Check Wix's current feature pages and app market for what you need.

**Try it before you pay.** Revora gives you 3 days of full access with no card. Setup is $350, your first month is free, then it is $100 a month, and you can cancel anytime. [See pricing](/pricing) or [start your system](/get-started).
